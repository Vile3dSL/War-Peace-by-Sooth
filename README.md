# WarTimers (Sooth Edition)

A lightweight, draggable HUD for ArcheAge that tracks zone states (War, Peace, Conflict, Danger) for Nuia, Haranya, and Auroria.

## Features
- **Live Timers:** Accurate countdowns for state changes.
- **Color Coded:** Instantly see if a zone is in Peace (Blue), War (Red), or Conflict (Orange).
- **Position Saving:** The window saves its position on screen automatically.
- **Tabs:** Switch easily between Nuia, Haranya, and Auroria zones.
- **Minimized Mode:** Click the `-` button to shrink the window when you don't need the full list.

## How to Use

- **Move the Window:** Hold **SHIFT + Left Click and Drag** to move the HUD.
- **Save Position:** The position saves automatically when you stop dragging.
- **Minimize:** Click the small `-` button in the top right to hide the list.
- **Switch Regions:** Click the buttons (Nuia, Haranya, Auroria) to change the zone list.
